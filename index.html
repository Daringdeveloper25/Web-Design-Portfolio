Lyric Poster: Live To Win

This poster represents thematic elements drawn from song lyrics, translating the song’s themes and imagery into a cohesive visual composition. For instance, I included Paul Stanley, the singer of “Live to Win,” alongside Rocky Balboa and the exaggerated character designs from South Park’s World of Warcraft episode. The South Park characters were specifically chosen to reference the “Live to Win” gaming montage featured in the episode, visually reinforcing the song’s themes of perseverance, dedication, and obsession.
